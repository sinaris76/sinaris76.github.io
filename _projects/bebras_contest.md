As a Scientific Team Member in the Bebras Contest at Fatemi Cultural Institute (2022), I collaborated with Professor Masoud Seddighin to design computational thinking tasks for the international contest.

## Achievement

Our computational thinking task was selected as the **Best Task** at the International Bebras Challenge in North Macedonia (Virtual), chosen from among more than 150 tasks submitted internationally.

## Responsibilities

- **Task Design**: Created new computational thinking problems for the international contest
- **Translation & Categorization**: Translated and categorized international problems for the national contest
- **Contest Organization**: Supervised the proper execution of the contest

## Impact

The Bebras Contest promotes computational thinking among students worldwide. Our award-winning task contributed to this mission by providing an engaging and educational challenge that helped students develop their computational thinking skills.

## Technical Details

- **Institution**: Fatemi Cultural Institute
- **Year**: 2022
- **Collaborator**: Prof. Masoud Seddighin
- **Award**: Best Task, International Bebras Challenge, North Macedonia
