This research project was conducted during my internship at Aalto University, Finland, working with Aurelien Nioche under Professor Antti Oulasvirta in the HCI lab.

## Research Focus

The project explored how Deep Reinforcement Learning (DeepRL) could be used to create more effective AI teaching agents, specifically for teaching second language vocabulary. The key innovation was incorporating cognitive characteristics of learners into the teaching strategy.

## My Contributions

- **Curriculum Learning Implementation**: Developed and implemented Curriculum Learning methods to optimize the teaching sequence
- **Environment Design**: Designed the learning environment to support adaptive teaching
- **Experimentation & Visualization**: Created novel experimental setups and visualizations to improve agent performance
- **Cognitive Modeling**: Integrated learner cognitive characteristics into the RL framework

## Impact

This work contributed to the field of AIED by demonstrating how RL-based approaches can create more personalized and effective educational experiences, taking into account individual learner differences.
