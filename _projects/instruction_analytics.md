As a Research Assistant at UC Irvine (2023), I worked on analyzing how instruction design impacts educational equity and student engagement using large-scale learning management system (LMS) data.

## Research Questions

- How does instruction design affect student engagement?
- What is the relationship between instruction design and educational equity?
- How can we use contextual big data from LMS to improve instruction?

## Methodology

- **Data Source**: Contextual big data from learning management systems
- **Analysis**: Statistical analysis of instruction design patterns and student outcomes
- **Focus Areas**: Equity, engagement, and learning outcomes

## Impact

This research contributes to understanding how instructional design decisions can promote equity and improve student engagement in online learning environments.

## Technical Details

- **Institution**: University of California, Irvine
- **Duration**: 2023
- **Role**: Research Assistant
